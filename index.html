Yield Formula

Each calendar day, the fund accrues interest at the ECB Euro Short-Term Rate (€STR) plus 8.5 bps (`0.085%`) on an ACT/360 money-market basis, minus the 0.10% annual TER (ACT/365): 

r_day = (€STR + 0.085%) / 360 − (TER / 365)
V_end = V_0 × ∏(1 + r_day) − Tax − StampDuty − Fees

Tax note: In Italy, because XEON's collateral basket is predominantly composed of EU white-list government bonds, capital gains are typically taxed at a blended effective rate near ~12.94%–13.5% instead of 26%, depending on the semi-annual white-list ratio published by the broker.
